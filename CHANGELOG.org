* V1 -- 2026-05-28 (Initial release)
** Added
- Raw and cleaned DSC/Flash-DSC data for C/PEKK crystallization study.
- Thermal camera surface temperature profiles for static laser heating.
- SEM-EDX line scans and images for chemical surface characterization.
- Mandrel peel test data for bond strength evaluation.
- High-resolution microscopy and fractography images.
- Python analysis and plotting scripts.
- Comprehensive documentation in Org-mode and BibTeX formats.
